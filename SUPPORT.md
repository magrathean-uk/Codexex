# Codexex support

Product information and the App Store support destination are at [codexex.eu](https://codexex.eu/). For support, use [contact+codexex@magrathean.uk](mailto:contact+codexex@magrathean.uk), the address listed in [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md). Licensing enquiries use [contact@magrathean.uk](mailto:contact@magrathean.uk). Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Useful details

Include the app version and build, macOS or iOS version, affected screen, steps to reproduce, expected result, and actual result. Say whether the issue occurs in Preview Mode or with a signed-in account, and whether a manual refresh changes it. Redact account details from screenshots.

For an iOS Live Activity issue, include the displayed update time and whether opening the app and refreshing updates the quota. Background refresh timing is controlled by iOS; a stale card alone does not identify which part failed.

For local usage issues on macOS, describe which view is missing data without attaching raw session logs. Session logs and hook metadata can contain project paths, identifiers, and private work.

Do not send access or refresh tokens, passwords, signing files, or unredacted diagnostics. Share only the material needed to explain the problem.

## Understanding quota readings

Codexex displays third-party quota responses and local calculations. Readings can be stale, rounded, delayed, incomplete, or unavailable. Use your OpenAI account records for billing or contractual questions. Local session token totals and account quota percentages measure different things.
