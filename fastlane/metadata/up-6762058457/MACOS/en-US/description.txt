Codexex is a native macOS menu bar utility for tracking Codex usage at a glance.

The compact popup shows the next 5-hour reset when your account reports one, or weekly allowance and progress when it does not. Weekly reset timing is always close at hand.

The compact menu-bar view uses the OpenAI mark and your reported 5-hour window when available, or your weekly percentage otherwise.

Codexex keeps the menu bar popup and Settings focused on a clean, readable native Mac experience.

Codexex supports ChatGPT sign-in, Preview Mode for App Review and offline testing, and a compact helper/XPC release flow that keeps the app self-contained. Optional local-session analysis reads official Codex session logs on device and does not use browser cookies or scraping.
