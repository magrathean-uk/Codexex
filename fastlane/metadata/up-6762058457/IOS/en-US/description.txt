Codexex is a native iPhone and iPad utility for keeping Codex usage readable at a glance.

It shows 5-hour and weekly usage, reset timing, Peaks/Cycle/Month history, pace and forecast hints, and Preview Mode sample data so you can check the layout before signing in.

Version 4 refreshes the UI with calmer spacing, current usage-first labels, bottom Settings/Refresh controls on iOS, and a cleaner light/dark theme across the app.

Codexex supports ChatGPT sign-in through Safari and a device-code flow. Quota reads stay on this device. No Mac bridge, no browser cookies, and no scraping.
