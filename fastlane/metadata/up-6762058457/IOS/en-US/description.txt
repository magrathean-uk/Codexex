Codexex is a native iPhone and iPad utility for keeping Codex usage readable at a glance.

It shows the next 5-hour reset when your account reports one, plus weekly allowance and reset timing. When only a weekly window is reported, weekly allowance and progress lead instead. Preview Mode lets you check the layout before signing in.

Start a compact Live Activity after a foreground refresh to see your weekly percentage left and progress bar from the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island.

Codexex keeps the iPhone and iPad experience focused on clear quota status and quick refresh controls.

Codexex supports ChatGPT sign-in through Safari and a device-code flow. Quota reads stay on this device, with no browser cookies and no scraping.
